Master Introduction & Scope of Governance

This Master Legal & Governance Framework governs all access to, interaction with, and services provided by God of Automation ("we", "us", "our"). By booking an architectural review, submitting an intake form, commissioning a digital build, or entering into any service agreement with us, you ("Client", "User", "Purchaser") formally acknowledge, accept, and agree to be bound by the terms, policies, and governance protocols outlined herein.

God of Automation provides high-performance custom websites, conversion funnels, cognitive AI automation pipelines, and enterprise backend systems. Our operational scope serves commercial enterprises (B2B), consumer-facing brands (B2C), and individual clients alike, subject to the conditions set forth in this document.

Universal Client Scope: Whether deploying a multi-agent orchestration for a multinational enterprise or a focused automation pipeline for an independent operator, our governance standards maintain absolute engineering rigour and deliverable transparency.
02

Service Delivery & Engagement Lifecycle

All engagements follow a structured operational pipeline designed to protect both client capital and engineering integrity:

Initial Review & Discovery

Every project commences with a comprehensive system review to isolate workflow bottlenecks and define exact requirements.

Scoped Action Plans

Prior to the initiation of engineering work, a fixed scope, timeline, and deliverable schedule are presented for client approval. No code or asset production begins without mutual sign-off.

Execution & Knowledge Transfer

Systems are engineered using elite architecture standards (including n8n, LangGraph, Supabase, and Vercel edge deployment), followed by structured training and documentation handover.

Final Handover

Upon completion and settlement of financial obligations, absolute ownership and operational control are transferred to the client.

03

Financial Governance & Payment Structures

To accommodate diverse procurement models across enterprises and individuals, financial terms are structured under specific, binding parameters:

3.1 Billing Models

  • 100% Upfront Payment: Selected projects or micro-sprints may operate under a full upfront payment structure to immediately lock in engineering resources and execution priority.
  • 50/50 Milestone Split: Standard projects operate on a milestone structure comprising a 50% initiation deposit required prior to engineering commencement, and a 50% final balance due immediately prior to system deployment and codebase handover.

3.2 Non-Refundable Policy & Deployment Controls

Due to the highly customized, resource-intensive nature of software engineering, custom workflow design, and AI infrastructure setup, all deposits and payments are strictly non-refundable once engineering work has formally commenced.

Deployment & Handover Controls: For 50/50 milestone projects, final system deployment, domain connection pointing, production webhook activation, and repository transfer are strictly withheld until the final 50% balance is settled in full.
04

Post-Launch Maintenance, Support & Retainer Protocols

Once a digital asset or automation pipeline is deployed, ongoing stability and modifications are governed by the following operational rules:

4.1 Monthly Retainers & Proactive Management

Clients may opt into ongoing monthly maintenance retainers. Retainer agreements secure dedicated engineering hours for proactive system optimization, performance monitoring, security patching, and minor iterations.

4.2 Complimentary Minor Adjustments

Minimal adjustments, minor visual touch-ups, or minor bug fixes identified immediately post-launch may be handled on a complimentary basis solely at the discretion of God of Automation.

4.3 Billed Major Modifications & Overhauls

Extensive architecture updates, major functional overhauls, or system failures resulting from external API alterations, unauthorized client-side code modifications, or third-party platform policy shifts that require significant engineering hours, specialized developer tools, or computing resources will be subject to standard professional service charges.

05

Intellectual Property & Codebase Ownership

5.1 Client Ownership Rights

Upon receipt of final, cleared payment, custom website code, JSON automation workflows, database schemas, and conversion funnels built specifically for your project become the absolute property of your organization or person.

5.2 Proprietary Agency Frameworks

God of Automation retains permanent, unencumbered ownership of underlying generic software templates, pre-existing code libraries, proprietary boilerplate frameworks, and foundational architectural methodologies utilized to accelerate development across multiple client builds.

06

Client Responsibilities & Access Dependencies

To ensure project delivery adheres to estimated timelines, the client agrees to fulfill the following operational responsibilities:

  • Third-Party Credentials: Provide timely access, credentials, and API tokens for required third-party platforms and tools (e.g., n8n, Notion, Calendly, CRMs, cloud providers, domain registrars).
  • Review & Asset Delivery: Provide prompt feedback, asset delivery, and milestone sign-offs during review phases. God of Automation accepts no liability for project schedule extensions caused by client unresponsiveness or delayed access.
07

Limitation of Liability & Third-Party Dependencies

God of Automation constructs fault-tolerant, resilient systems, but relies heavily on third-party cloud infrastructure and software ecosystems (such as Vercel, Supabase, Notion, Calendly, and external Large Language Model providers).

  • Third-Party Outages: We accept no liability or financial responsibility for operational disruptions, downtime, data loss, API throttling, rate limits, or sudden policy shifts originating from third-party vendors or external platform providers.
  • Liability Ceiling: In no event shall God of Automation’s total cumulative liability exceed the total professional fees actually paid by the client for the specific project or service engagement giving rise to the claim.
08

Data Privacy & Confidentiality Governance

Both parties agree to treat all proprietary business data, trade secrets, operational strategies, and API credentials shared during the engagement as strictly confidential.

  • Data Minimization: We collect only the essential telemetry, credentials, and business inputs required to architect and deploy your automated systems.
  • Age Restrictions & Minors: Our services and digital properties are strictly intended for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data relating to minors. Any such data discovered within our intake pipelines will be permanently and immediately purged.
09

Engagement Termination Protocols

Either party may terminate a service agreement in writing if the opposing party commits a material breach of these governance terms.

If an active project is terminated mid-stream by the client, all engineering hours completed up to the date of written termination remain fully billable, and all previously paid deposits remain non-refundable.
