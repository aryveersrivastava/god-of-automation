Introduction & Acceptance of Terms

These Terms of Service ("Terms") govern your access to and use of the artificial intelligence infrastructure, workflow automation design, and system architecture services provided by God of Automation ("we", "us", "our"). By booking an architecture audit, submitting an intake form, or entering into a service agreement with us, you agree to be bound by these Terms.

Universal Client Scope: God of Automation provides tailored workflow automation and AI infrastructure services to commercial enterprises (B2B), consumer-facing brands (B2C), and individual clients alike.
02

Scope of Services & Project Execution

We architect custom automated pipelines, lead routing engines, stateful multi-agent systems, and enterprise data integrations.

  • Custom Engineering: Every project is custom-scoped based on your exact requirements, whether for a growing business or an individual workflow.
  • Mutual Blueprint Alignment: The exact deliverables, timelines, and technical architecture specifications will be mutually agreed upon prior to the initiation of engineering work.
03

Payment Structures & Financial Terms

To accommodate varying client needs, we utilize flexible billing structures determined during project scoping:

Payment Structures

Projects may operate under a 100% Upfront Payment model or a 50/50 Milestone Split (comprising a 50% initiation deposit to begin engineering, and a 50% final balance due immediately prior to system deployment and codebase handover).

Non-Refundable Fees

Due to the custom, resource-intensive nature of software engineering and systems architecture, all deposits and payments are strictly non-refundable once engineering work has commenced.

Delayed Balances

For 50/50 milestone projects, final system deployment and deployment keys will be withheld until the 50% final balance is settled in full.

04

Post-Launch Maintenance, Support & Retainers

Once a system is deployed and handed over, ongoing system stability is managed under the following terms:

  • Retainer Agreements: Clients may opt-in to ongoing monthly maintenance retainers for proactive system optimization, performance monitoring, and updates.
  • Complimentary Minor Adjustments: Minimal adjustments, minor bug fixes, or minor configuration tweaks identified immediately post-launch may be handled on a complimentary basis at our sole discretion.
  • Billed Major Modifications: Major system modifications, extensive architecture overhauls, or catastrophic failures resulting from external API changes, unauthorized client-side code edits, or third-party platform alterations that require significant engineering hours, developer tools, or resources will be subject to standard professional service charges.
05

Intellectual Property & Ownership

Client Ownership

Upon receipt of final payment, custom workflows, JSON pipelines, and database configurations built specifically for your use case become the property of your organization or person.

Proprietary Frameworks

God of Automation retains ownership of underlying generic software templates, pre-existing code libraries, and foundational architectural methodologies utilized to accelerate development.

To ensure projects are delivered within estimated timelines, the client agrees to:

  • Credential & Platform Access: Provide timely access to required third-party platforms, APIs, CRMs, and workspace credentials (e.g., n8n, Notion, Calendly, Google Workspace).
  • Prompt Collaboration: Provide prompt feedback, asset delivery, and approvals during milestone reviews. God of Automation is not liable for project delays resulting from unresponsiveness or delayed client-side access.

Limitation of Liability & Third-Party Dependencies

God of Automation builds fault-tolerant, resilient systems, but we rely on third-party cloud infrastructure (such as Vercel, Notion, Calendly, Google, and external LLM providers).

Third-Party Outages: We accept no liability for operational disruptions, downtime, data loss, or financial damages caused by outages, API changes, rate limits, or policy shifts originating from third-party vendors or platforms.

In no event shall God of Automation’s total cumulative liability exceed the total professional fees actually paid by the client for the specific project giving rise to the claim.
