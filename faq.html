SECTION 03

Financials, Ownership & Post-Launch Support

We offer two distinct deployment models to fit your operational style and budget:

Absolute Ownership

We build the infrastructure directly under your accounts. You pay the platform providers (e.g., Vercel, OpenAI, n8n) directly with zero agency markup. When we are done, you own it 100%.

Fully Managed Monthly

For clients who want a completely hands-off "done-for-you" experience, we offer a bundled monthly retainer. In this model, we host the architecture, cover the infrastructure costs, and actively maintain the system for one flat monthly fee.

Every deployment comes standard with a 14-Day Hyper-Care Warranty. For two weeks post-launch, any minor tweaks, bug fixes, or operational adjustments are handled by our engineering team completely free of charge. After 14 days, you take full sovereign control of your system—or you can choose to hire us on an ongoing monthly retainer to manage, monitor, and scale your pipelines proactively.

God of Automation engineers digital solutions for commercial enterprises (B2B), consumer-facing brands (B2C), and individual high-ticket founders. If you have manual bottlenecks costing you time and capital, we can build the architecture to automate it.
