Section 1: Enterprise Infrastructure & B2B Solutions

We do not rely on multi-tenant, public SaaS dashboards where your data is co-mingled. For enterprise deployments, we architect self-hosted instances of n8n and utilize Supabase (PostgreSQL) for isolated data warehousing. Furthermore, our Autonomous Hospital OS utilizes time-bound, cryptographically signed pre-signed URLs for lab reports that automatically expire after 7 days, ensuring strict compliance with global health data standards.

We eliminate AI hallucinations through deterministic engineering. We do not plug raw LLMs directly into your customer-facing channels. We use LangGraph and LangChain to create multi-agent reasoning loops, equipped with strict JSON output schemas, negative prompt constraints, and verification nodes. If the AI output fails validation against your company's knowledge base, the payload is routed to a human fallback or Dead Letter Queue (DLQ) rather than sent to the client.

Upon final payment and project handover, you own the deployment environment, the infrastructure architecture, and the associated data structures. You are not paying us a monthly "per-seat" SaaS tax. You own the sovereign system; we simply engineer and deploy it.
