Strict Privacy Standards

Commitment to Data Protection

God of Automation ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal digital data. This Privacy Policy outlines our data processing practices in strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India, and global privacy standards.

DPDP Act Mandate: We act as a responsible Data Fiduciary, ensuring transparency, explicit lawful consent, zero non-consensual processing, and prompt honoring of your digital rights.

1. The Data We Collect & Lawful Purpose

We practice strict Data Minimization. We only collect digital personal data necessary to execute our technical services:

  • For Career Automation Clients: We collect your raw career dossiers, educational history, and contact information solely for the purpose of engineering your ATS-optimized resume.
  • For Enterprise Clients: We process technical payloads, webhooks, and API keys necessary to build and monitor your autonomous pipelines.
  • For Website Visitors: We collect basic analytics and device information to optimize UI/UX performance.

2. How We Process Data with AI

We are an AI-first agency. When you utilize our services, your data may be processed by Large Language Models (LLMs).

Zero-Retention APIs

We utilize enterprise-grade API endpoints for OpenAI, Google Gemini, and Anthropic Claude. Your personal data and enterprise logic are explicitly opted out of model training.

Infrastructure Hosting

Client data pipelines managed by us are hosted on secure instances of Supabase (PostgreSQL) and n8n, utilizing industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit.

3. Data Fiduciary Obligations & Third-Party Sharing

As a Data Fiduciary, we do not sell, rent, or broker your personal data to third parties. Data is only shared with essential sub-processors (e.g., Stripe for payments, AWS/Supabase for hosting, LLM providers for processing) required to fulfill our contractual obligations.

4. Your Rights as a Data Principal (DPDP Act 2023)

Under the DPDP Act, you (the Data Principal) possess the following enforceable rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of the personal data we process.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You may request the modification of inaccurate data or the deletion of your data once the processing purpose is fulfilled.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your consent for data processing at any time by contacting our support team. Note that withdrawing consent may terminate your ability to use certain automated pipelines.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have the right to register a complaint regarding data processing.

5. Data Retention & Breach Notification

We retain raw career data (for DFY Rebuild clients) for a maximum of 30 days post-delivery to facilitate any revisions, after which the raw payload is purged from our active systems.

In the highly unlikely event of a security breach affecting your personal digital data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India without undue delay, as mandated by law.
