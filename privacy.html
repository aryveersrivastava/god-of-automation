01

Introduction & Controller Details

God of Automation ("we", "us", "our") operates as a premium AI infrastructure and systems architecture practice. Legally, we operate as a registered Sole Proprietorship. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

This Privacy Policy informs you how we look after your personal data when you visit our website (regardless of where you visit it from) and tells you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you. Under global data protection frameworks, God of Automation acts as the Data Controller for the information collected through our digital properties.

02

Categorization of Processed Data

We operate a minimalist, sovereign data architecture and strictly adhere to data minimization principles. We may collect, use, store, and transfer different kinds of personal data about you, which we have grouped together as follows:

Identity & Contact Data

Includes your company name, job title, work email address, and scheduling preferences. This is collected when you request an architecture audit or book a consultation via our Calendly integration.

Technical Architecture Data

Includes details about your current operational bottlenecks, CRM systems, inbound lead volume, and infrastructure challenges submitted through our intake forms.

Telemetry & Usage Data

Includes anonymous, aggregated website usage metrics (e.g., page views, time on page, geographical region). We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) or track IP addresses for this purpose.

03

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data strictly for operational and architectural purposes under the specific legal bases outlined by the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and equivalent international laws:

  • Contractual Necessity (Article 6(1)(b)): Processing is necessary to respond to your inquiries, map your infrastructure, and execute pre-contractual measures prior to entering into a service agreement.
  • Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)): Processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, including pursuing B2B commercial leads, sending architectural follow-ups, and managing our client relationships.
  • System Security & Performance (Article 6(1)(f)): Legitimate interests in preventing automated bot abuse, ensuring network security, and maintaining high-performance digital edge delivery.
04

Enterprise Subprocessors & Third-Party Sharing

Your data is not our product. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

We utilize a strictly vetted stack of enterprise-grade subprocessors to route and secure your data:

Hosting & Edge

Vercel

Edge routing, SSL encryption, and secure hosting infrastructure.

Telemetry

Plausible Analytics

Privacy-first, cookie-less website telemetry and performance tracking.

Scheduling

Calendly

Automated scheduling and timezone mapping (acting as Data Processor).

Email

Google Workspace (Gmail)

Secure, TLS-encrypted enterprise email transport.

Integration

n8n

Automated JSON webhook orchestration (processing data strictly in transit).

Internal Logging

Notion

Secure, encrypted internal CRM and database logging.

05

Data Retention & Sovereignty

We only retain your personal data for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes we collected it for, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, or reporting requirements.

  • Contact Submissions & CRM Lead Data: Retained for a maximum of 24 months from the date of submission or last interaction, unless you request earlier deletion.
  • Server Access Logs: Automatically purged after 30 days.
  • Scheduling Data: Retained in accordance with Calendly’s enterprise data retention policy.
  • Site Analytics: Plausible Analytics retains zero individual user profiles; all metrics are aggregated and anonymized upon collection.
06

International Data Transfers

Our infrastructure is distributed globally. Because we utilize cloud subprocessors located in the United States and the European Union, your data may be transferred to, stored, and processed in jurisdictions outside your country of residence.

We ensure a similar degree of protection is afforded to it by ensuring these systems operate under stringent GDPR-compliant safeguards, including the implementation of Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and zero-trust security models.

07

Your Rights Under Global Frameworks

We recognize and enforce your rights under comprehensive global privacy frameworks, including the GDPR (EU/UK), the CCPA/CPRA (California), the PIPEDA (Canada), and the DPDP Act (India). You maintain the absolute right to:

Request Access

Receive a full export of the personal data we hold about you.

Request Rectification

Correct incomplete or inaccurate architectural data we hold about you.

Request Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

Request the immediate deletion of your records from our Notion database and active pipelines where there is no good reason for us continuing to process it.

Object to Processing

Object to our processing of your personal data where we are relying on a legitimate interest.

Request Restriction

Suspend the processing of your personal data in specific scenarios.

Request Portability

Transfer your data to a third party in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, initiate a request by emailing our designated Data Protection Officer. We execute all valid requests within 30 days free of charge.

08

Cookies and Tracking (Cookie-Less Infrastructure)

Unlike standard digital agencies, God of Automation does not rely on invasive tracking pixels, cross-site trackers, or Google Analytics. We run a privacy-first infrastructure.

  • Analytics: We utilize Plausible Analytics, an open-source tool that tracks site performance without utilizing cookies, generating persistent identifiers, or collecting personal data.
  • Third-Party Widgets: Calendly may load strictly necessary session cookies only when you actively interact with the booking widget to secure your scheduling session.
  • Consent: Because we do not deploy non-essential tracking cookies or profiling technologies, we are not legally required to display a disruptive cookie consent banner.
09

Security Architecture & Safeguards

We secure your data with the same architectural rigor we apply to our clients' automated systems. We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way, altered, or disclosed. This includes enforced HTTPS/SSL encryption in transit, strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) within our Notion CRM, and utilizing encrypted JSON payloads through n8n to eliminate manual handling errors.

10

Data Breach Notification Procedures

In the unlikely event of a structural data breach or unauthorized access to our CRM infrastructure, we have put in place procedures to deal with any suspected personal data breach. We will notify you and any applicable regulator of a breach where we are legally required to do so within 72 hours of discovery.

11

Third-Party Links

Our website may include links to third-party websites, plug-ins, or applications. Clicking on those links or enabling those connections may allow third parties to collect or share data about you. We do not control these third-party websites and are not responsible for their privacy statements.

12

Commercial Focus & Children's Privacy

God of Automation engineers B2B infrastructure exclusively for commercial enterprises and professionals. Our services are not intended for individuals acting in a personal capacity. We do not knowingly collect personal data relating to children or anyone under the age of 18. Any such data discovered in our pipelines will be immediately permanently purged.

13

We continuously upgrade our legal and technical infrastructure. This version was last updated on the Effective Date listed above. Material changes to this data processing policy will be communicated directly via email to any entity residing in our active CRM within the past 24 months.
